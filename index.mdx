EdgeTimer is a barbershop appointment scheduling platform that brings clients and barbers together in one seamless app. Clients can browse barbers, book appointments, and rate their experience — while barbers manage their availability, confirm bookings, and build their reputation.

## How EdgeTimer works

EdgeTimer has two user roles — **clients** (clientes) and **barbers** (barberos) — each with their own workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create an account">
    Clients register directly in the app. Barbers are onboarded by the barbershop administrator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and connect">
    Clients browse the barber catalog, view specialties and ratings, then choose a barber to book with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book an appointment">
    Clients either request a custom appointment time or reserve a pre-opened availability slot from a barber.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage and complete">
    Barbers accept or decline requests, both sides can reschedule or cancel, and clients rate completed appointments.
  </Step>
</Steps>
