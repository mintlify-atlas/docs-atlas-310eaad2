---
title: "Get started as a client"
description: "Create your EdgeTimer account, browse available barbers, and book your first barbershop appointment with a client or slot request."
---

EdgeTimer lets clients book barbershop appointments directly from the mobile app. You can either request a custom appointment time with any barber in the catalog, or reserve a pre-opened availability slot. This guide walks you through account creation and your first booking.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Launch the EdgeTimer mobile app on your device. You will land on the role selection screen, which is the entry point for all users.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose your role">
    On the role screen, tap **Usuario** to continue as a client. This tells the app which home experience and permissions to load for your session.

    <Note>
      If you tap **Barbero** by mistake, use the "Volver a roles" link on the login screen to go back and select the correct role.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create your account">
    Tap **Registrarse** on the login screen to open the registration form. Fill in the following fields:

    | Field | Description |
    |---|---|
    | `nombre` | Your full name, displayed on appointment cards |
    | `email` | A valid email address used to log in |
    | `telefono` | Your phone number (optional) |
    | `password` | A password of at least 6 characters |

    <Warning>
      Passwords shorter than 6 characters will be rejected. Make sure your password meets the minimum length before submitting.
    </Warning>

    Once submitted, your account is created immediately. You will be taken to the login screen to sign in with your new credentials.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse barbers">
    After logging in, the **Home** screen shows two sections:

    - **Horarios disponibles** — availability slots that barbers have pre-opened. Each card shows the barber's name, date, and start time.
    - **Barberos AlphaCorte** — the full barber catalog with photos, specialties, working hours, and average ratings.

    Tap a barber card to view their profile, or tap **Solicitar Cita** directly from the catalog to pre-select that barber when requesting an appointment.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Book an appointment">
    EdgeTimer offers two ways to book:

    **Option A — Reserve a slot:** Tap **Reservar** on any available slot card. In the modal that opens, select one or more procedures. The app calculates the estimated duration and end time automatically. Tap **Confirmar reserva** to complete the booking.

    **Option B — Request an appointment:** Tap **Solicitar nueva cita** (the primary action button at the top of the Home screen). In the request form, choose a barber, select procedures, pick a date (`fecha` in `YYYY-MM-DD` format), and choose a desired start time (`horaInicio`). Tap **Enviar solicitud** to send the request to the barber.

    <Tip>
      Appointment requests must finish before 18:00. The app shows the estimated end time as you select procedures, so you can adjust your start time if needed.
    </Tip>

    After submitting a **request** (Option A), your appointment appears in the Agenda with a **pendiente** (pending) status until the barber accepts or rejects it. After **reserving a slot** (Option B), the appointment is immediately **confirmada** — no barber approval needed because the slot was already published as available.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Track your appointments in Agenda">
    Open the **Agenda** tab to manage all your appointments. The Agenda is organized into tabs:

    - **Proximas** — confirmed upcoming appointments
    - **Solicitadas** — pending requests, rejected, and cancelled appointments
    - **Pasadas** — completed appointments, where you can leave a rating
    - **Calendario** — a monthly calendar view showing your bookings at a glance

    From the Agenda you can also reschedule or cancel an appointment, as long as it is at least one day away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
